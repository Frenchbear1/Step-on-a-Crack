Step on a crack…

…break Grandma's back. She's hobbling right behind you with her cane, and she will feel every one. Turn your sound on.

  • Collect stamps from all 7 places in town, then bring them home to Grandma's.
  • Watch the shoe outline ahead of your feet. If it turns red, your next step lands on a crack.
  • Jump over cracks, or tiptoe in short, careful steps.
  • Dr. Spine Chiropractic fixes one break every 30 seconds. Five breaks and Grandma folds like a lawn chair.

Keyboard: W/S walk forward and back, A/D turn, Space to jump, Shift to tiptoe. Drag the mouse to look around, scroll to zoom, double-click to snap back behind you.
Touch: drag on the left half to walk (a gentle push tiptoes), drag on the right half to look around, pinch to zoom.
