# Step on a Crack

Step on a crack, break your grandma's back. A 3D browser game: walk a small town collecting stamps from 7 places while Grandma hobbles beside you. Every crack you step on, she feels it.

Play by opening `index.html` in a browser (it loads three.js r128 from cdnjs).

## Controls
- **Keyboard:** W/S walk, A/D turn, Space jump, Shift tiptoe. Drag the mouse to look around, scroll to zoom, double-click to snap behind.
- **Touch:** drag the left half to walk (gentle push = tiptoe), drag the right half to look, pinch to zoom.

Dr. Spine Chiropractic fixes one break every 30 seconds. Five breaks and Grandma folds like a lawn chair.
